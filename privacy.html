Privacy Policy

Luggage Loading Simulator · Android app com.luggageloading.simulator
Last updated: 21 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This is an English translation of the Turkish Gizlilik Politikası. If the two differ, the Turkish text prevails.

This policy explains what information the Luggage Loading Simulator game (the “Game”) collects, why, and what rights you have. In short: the Game has no accounts, no ads, no payments and no user tracking. Your progress stays on your phone. The only information that reaches us is technical error and crash reports, and feedback messages you choose to send.

1. Who we are and how to reach us

Developer and data controller: {{VERI_SORUMLUSU}}
Contact: bagajyukleme@gmail.com

2. Information that stays on your phone (not sent to us)

The Game stores the following only on your device, in the app's own storage: level progress, stars and scores, cosmetics bought with in-game points, Flight Pass progress, the name you give your operator character (up to 14 characters), language, sound and display settings, and optional “Phone measurement” records. None of this is sent to us. Measurement results are copied to your clipboard only when you tap “Copy results”; where you paste them is up to you.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this app data in the backup of your own Google account. That backup is kept by Google in your account; we have no access to it. Uninstalling the app, or Settings › Reset progress, deletes the progress on the device.

3. Information we receive

3.1 Error and crash reports (automatic)

To improve the Game we use the error and crash reporting service Sentry. When an error, crash, freeze (ANR) or slow screen transition happens, and as a session record each time the app starts, the following technical information is sent:

  • technical details of the error or crash (error message, stack trace, in-app steps before the event);
  • app version and build number;
  • device information: manufacturer, model, Android version and build, screen size and density, battery level, orientation, and technical context such as memory and connection state;
  • a random identifier generated for this installation of the app (not your name, phone number or advertising ID; a new one is created if you reinstall);
  • your IP address during the connection. The IP address is not stored in the reports; Sentry may derive an approximate location (country and city) from it.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID. Performance tracing and session replay are off.

3.2 Feedback messages (your choice)

If you send a message from Settings › Feedback, we receive: your message (up to 1,000 characters), the type you chose (bug or idea), the Game's language, how many levels you have finished, the last level you played, the screen you were on, and the device and version information above. For a bug message, an error report from the last 10 minutes may be linked to it. There is no email field; if you want a reply, you can write your email address in your message. Please don't include other personal information.

4. How we use it

  • to find and fix errors and crashes and keep the Game running smoothly on different phones;
  • to read your feedback, reply if you asked us to, and improve the Game.

We do not use this information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Legal bases (where the GDPR or similar laws apply): our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and fixing faults (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for crash reports, and your request plus our legitimate interest for feedback.

5. Who we share it with

The information is processed only by our service provider Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) on our behalf and on our instructions. Our Sentry account is in Sentry's EU data region; reports are stored in its data centre in Germany (Frankfurt). Sentry is a US company and may access data from other countries for support. For users in Türkiye, this transfer abroad is made under Article 9 of the KVKK using the standard contract published by the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board; for users in the EEA/UK, Sentry's data processing agreement includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Sentry's privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy.

We do not share or sell the information to anyone else, except where required by law or a valid request from a competent authority.

If you downloaded the Game from Google Play, downloads and store transactions are covered by Google's own privacy policy.

6. How long we keep it

Error, crash and feedback reports are kept in Sentry for at most 90 days and then deleted automatically. If we exchange emails about your feedback, those emails are kept until the matter is resolved and any legal retention period has passed.

7. Security

Information is sent from your phone to Sentry over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the Sentry account is limited to the developer.

8. Children

The Game is meant for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no accounts, chat or ads. If you are under 13, please don't put your email or other personal information in a feedback message. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or object to its processing. Crash reports are kept under a random installation ID, not your name, so to help us find a specific record, tell us the feedback message you sent or roughly when you sent it. Contact: bagajyukleme@gmail.com. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. Users in Türkiye have additional rights under the KVKK, described in the KVKK notice (Turkish).

10. App permissions

The Game only uses internet access and network state permissions, to send crash reports and feedback. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or file access. The Game also works offline; reports created offline are sent when you are back online.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page with a new effective date. For significant changes we will also let you know in the Game or on its store page.
